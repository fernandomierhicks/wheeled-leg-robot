Rationale
-Wide bearings 6804 bearings
-Double suspended tibia for max stiffness - less friction
-Single coupler for simplicity
-Movable Leg plate that bolts onto body for easy CG adjustment


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------Optimization
-Optimized for max travel with 2Nm limit when applying 1kg force on wheel. Leave us some margin
-10mm max vertical deviation ( to avoid balancing issues when changing leg height
-ok for femur to collie with coupler, design has them in different planes 


See 2N_10mm_279mm.pdf for main dimensions. 
Stroke is +28 to -57 degrees.
